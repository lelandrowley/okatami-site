Okatami — Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · Applies to the Okatami app for iPhone. The same policy appears inside the app under Library → Settings → Privacy, and the two are kept in step.

Okatami does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no third-party services.

What stays on your device

Your stories, drafts, and their details (the time saved, the tag's identifier and type, and location coordinates if you've turned that on) are stored in Okatami's private storage on your iPhone. They stay until you delete them: swipe a story in the Library to delete it, or delete Okatami to remove all of it. They're included in your normal iPhone backup, which Apple manages, not Okatami. iCloud Backup is encrypted by Apple; a backup to a computer is encrypted if you turn that option on. The developer never receives a copy, so there is nothing to delete or hand over on request.

NFC tags

Okatami reads and writes tags only when you hold one to your iPhone and start a scan, never in the background. Anything you write to a tag is stored on the tag itself, in plain text, and anyone with an NFC-capable phone and a reader app can read it. Don't write something to a tag that you wouldn't want a stranger to find.

Location

Off by default. When you turn it on, Okatami asks iOS for permission to use your location while the app is open, and saves the approximate place (rounded to about a hundred metres) where a story was written or first scanned, alongside that story on your device. Okatami never uploads coordinates. You can turn it off at any time in Okatami's Settings or in iOS Settings.

A story with a location shows it on a small Apple Maps map. To draw that map, Apple Maps loads map imagery for the surrounding area, as a map in any app does, under Apple's own privacy policy. Tapping the map opens the Maps app.

Sharing and export

When you export your library, the file (HTML, JSON, or plain text) is created on your iPhone and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is entirely your choice, and Okatami never sees or receives it. Exports can include location coordinates when a story has them, so before you share one, Okatami offers to leave locations out.

Import

Importing a backup reads only the file you choose, an Okatami HTML or JSON export, and restores its stories into the library on your iPhone. Nothing leaves your device. If the file is stored in iCloud Drive or another cloud folder, iOS fetches it first, as it would for any app.

No tracking

No analytics, no crash reporters, no advertising, no third-party libraries that transmit anything. You are not tracked across apps or websites. There is no mailing list.

Children

Okatami needs no account and asks for no personal information, so it collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Okatami ever changes how it handles data, this page and the policy inside the app will be updated together, with a new effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
